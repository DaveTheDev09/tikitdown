How to download TikTok videos on iPad

  1. Open the TikTok app on your iPad and find the video you want to save.
  2. Tap the Share icon (the arrow) and select Copy link.
  3. Switch to Safari and open TikItDown. Paste the link into the download field.
  4. Tap Download and choose your preferred quality.
  5. When prompted, tap Download in the Safari confirmation dialog.

Where downloaded videos are saved on iPad

When you download a video through Safari on iPadOS, the file is saved to the Files app under the Downloads folder. To find it:

  1. Open the Files app on your iPad.
  2. Tap Browse at the bottom, then tap On My iPad.
  3. Open the Downloads folder - your video will be there.

To move the video to your Camera Roll, open the video in Files, tap the Share button, and select Save Video. The video will then appear in the Photos app.

Safari tips for downloading on iPad

  • Request Desktop Site - if the page looks odd, tap the aA icon in the address bar and select Request Desktop Website.
  • Allow downloads - the first time you download, Safari may ask for permission. Tap Allow to continue.
  • Downloads manager - tap the downloads icon in the Safari address bar (a circle with a downward arrow) to see active and completed downloads.
  • iPadOS 13+ - the Files app and Safari download manager require iPadOS 13 or later. If you're on an older version, update through Settings > General > Software Update.

Screen recording on iPad captures the watermark, your notifications, and all on-screen controls. The resulting video has lower quality than the original. TikItDown gives you the clean source file at its original resolution - no watermarks, no quality loss, no extra editing needed.
