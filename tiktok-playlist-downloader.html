What are TikTok playlists?

TikTok playlists are curated collections that creators organize around specific themes. A cooking creator might have playlists for "Quick Recipes," "Dessert Ideas," and "Kitchen Hacks." A fitness creator could organize content into "Workout Routines," "Meal Prep," and "Stretching." Playlists let viewers watch related videos in sequence without hunting through a creator's entire profile.

The problem is that TikTok has no built-in way to download an entire playlist. You'd have to open each video individually, copy its link, and download it one at a time. For a playlist with 30 videos, that's 30 separate copy-paste-download cycles.

How to download a TikTok playlist

  1. Open the TikTok app or website and navigate to the playlist you want to save.
  2. Tap the share button on the playlist and copy the playlist link.
  3. Paste the playlist URL into the TikItDown field above and press Download.
  4. TikItDown fetches every video in the playlist and presents them for download.
  5. Download each video individually or grab them all as a ZIP file.

The whole process replaces dozens of individual downloads with a single paste. Videos are saved in the order they appear in the playlist, with sequential file names so you can keep them organized.

Playlists vs favorites

TikTok has two types of collections that people often confuse:

  • Playlists - created by the video's owner, organized by theme, appear on the creator's profile. Anyone can view public playlists.
  • Favorites - personal collections that any user builds by saving videos they like. Only you can see your own favorites list.

TikItDown handles both. Paste a playlist URL to download a creator's organized collection. Paste a favorites URL to download your own saved videos. The download process is identical.
