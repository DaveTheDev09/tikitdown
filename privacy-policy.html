Privacy Policy

Short version: TikItDown does not create accounts, does not track you across the web, and does not keep a history of your downloads. Here is exactly what happens with the data involved in using the service.

What we process

When you paste a video link, it is sent to our server only to fetch the public video source. The link and its metadata are held in memory for the duration of the request and are not written to any permanent store. Download history is never saved.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, emails or phone numbers - you can use TikItDown fully anonymously.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies.
  • No analytics that follow you across other websites.

Server logs

Like any web service, our servers record basic request metadata (IP address, user agent, requested page, timestamps) for operational and abuse-prevention purposes. These logs are kept for a short retention period and are not used for profiling or advertising.

Third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and user agent when the fonts are requested. Google's privacy policy applies to that exchange. We do not use third-party advertising networks.

Local storage

Your browser may store a small value indicating your language preference. That value stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Children

TikItDown does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will replace this page with a new date at the bottom. Continued use of the service means you accept the current policy.
