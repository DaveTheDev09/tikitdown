Takedown requests

If you are a copyright holder and believe content accessed through TikItDown infringes your rights, email us at tikitdown@gmail.com and include:

Because TikItDown does not host content, the practical remedy for a public video is usually removal of the video from its original platform. Where we have any technical ability to prevent access, we will act as quickly as possible after a valid notice.
