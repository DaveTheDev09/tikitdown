How to Download TikTok Videos on PC

The complete guide to saving TikTok videos on your Windows PC — fast, clean, and free.

Why Use TikItDown on Your PC?

Downloading TikTok videos on a PC is actually easier than on a phone. You have a bigger screen, a physical keyboard for pasting links, and plenty of storage for saving large video files. TikItDown is designed to work seamlessly on Windows PCs using any modern browser.

Whether you are using Windows 10, Windows 11, or an older version, TikItDown runs entirely in your browser with no software to install. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Brave all work perfectly. You paste a TikTok link, click download, and the MP4 file saves directly to your computer.

This is especially useful if you want to organize a collection of TikTok videos, edit them on your computer, or simply have a larger screen to preview the content before saving.

Step-by-Step: Download TikTok Videos on PC

Step 1 — Copy the TikTok Link

Open TikTok in your browser or the TikTok desktop app. Find the video you want to download, click the Share button, and select Copy Link. You can also right-click the address bar and copy the URL directly.

Short links like vm.tiktok.com/… and full URLs both work perfectly with TikItDown. There is no need to clean up the URL or remove tracking parameters — TikItDown handles it automatically.

Step 2 — Paste into TikItDown

Open a new tab and go to the TikItDown website. Click the download input field and press Ctrl+V (or right-click and select Paste) to insert the link. Click the Download button to start processing.

TikItDown will analyze the link and prepare download options. You will see the video thumbnail, quality options, and available formats within a couple of seconds.

Step 3 — Save to Your PC

Click the Download Video button. Your browser will begin downloading the file immediately. Most browsers show a download progress indicator in the bottom bar or top-right corner.

By default, Chrome and Edge save downloads to your Downloads folder. You can change this in your browser settings: go to Settings > Downloads and set a custom download location.

Where Do Downloads Save on Windows PC?

On a Windows PC, TikTok video downloads typically go to:

  1. Open File Explorer (press Win+E).
  2. Navigate to your Downloads folder: C:\Users\[YourName]\Downloads
  3. Your TikTok video will be there as an MP4 file.

You can also change the default download location in your browser settings. For example, in Chrome, go to Settings > Downloads and click Change next to the location field. Many people prefer saving directly to a dedicated TikTok Videos folder on their Desktop or Documents.

Common PC Download Issues and Fixes

Download Blocked by Browser or Antivirus

Some browsers or antivirus software may flag video downloads as suspicious. If your download is blocked, check your browser's download settings and make sure video downloads are allowed. You may also need to whitelist TikItDown in your antivirus software.

Video File Corrupted

If the downloaded video won't play, it may have been interrupted during download. Try downloading again. For the best results, use Chrome or Edge, which handle large file downloads more reliably.

Download Location Unknown

If you cannot find your downloaded video, press Ctrl+J in Chrome or Edge to open the download history. Click Show in folder next to the video to open its location in File Explorer.
