How to Download TikTok Audio — Extract Sound and Music

TikTok is full of original sounds, trending songs, and viral audio clips. This guide shows you how to extract the audio track from any TikTok video as an MP3 file.

What audio you can extract

Every TikTok video has an audio track. This might be a song from TikTok's music library, original dialogue recorded by the creator, a voiceover, sound effects, or a combination of all of these. When you download the audio, you get whatever sound is in that specific video.

Some common use cases:

  • Trending sounds — save a viral sound before it disappears from the platform.
  • Original dialogue — extract a funny or memorable quote from a video.
  • Background music — get the song used in a video so you can find it elsewhere.
  • Sound effects — pull a specific sound for use in your own content.

Step-by-step: extract audio from TikTok

  1. Open the TikTok app or go to tiktok.com and find the video with the audio you want.
  2. Tap Share (the arrow icon), then tap Copy link.
  3. Paste the link into the TikItDown field above and press Download.
  4. Select the MP3 download option. The audio file saves to your device.

The process is identical on any device — iPhone, Android, Windows, or Mac. The MP3 file is ready to play in any music app or audio player.

TikItDown extracts the audio at the same quality it was encoded in the original video. If the creator used a high-quality audio source, you get a high-quality MP3. The bitrate and sample rate are preserved as closely as possible during extraction.
