What is a TikTok slideshow post?

A slideshow is a post made of several photos that TikTok plays as a short "video", usually over music or a voiceover. They are hugely popular for aesthetics, mood boards, recipe steps, and product shots - and they are exactly the kind of content people want to keep in their own gallery.
