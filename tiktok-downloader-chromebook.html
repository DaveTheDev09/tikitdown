TikTok Downloader for Chromebook - Chrome OS Guide

Download TikTok videos on your Chromebook using Chrome. No Linux or Android app needed - works entirely in the browser.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

How to download TikTok videos on Chromebook

  1. Open the TikTok app or website on your Chromebook and find the video.
  2. Copy the video URL from the address bar or use Share > Copy link.
  3. Open a new tab in Chrome and go to TikItDown.
  4. Paste the link in the download field and click Download.
  5. Choose the quality and the video will download to your Chromebook.

Where downloads are saved on Chromebook

Chrome OS saves all downloads to the Downloads folder by default. To access it:

  1. Click the Launcher (circle icon in the bottom-left corner).
  2. Search for and open the Files app.
  3. Navigate to Downloads in the left sidebar.

You can also press Alt+Shift+M to open the Files app directly.

Save to Google Drive

Chromebook users often rely on Google Drive for storage. To save a downloaded TikTok video to Drive:

  1. Open the Files app.
  2. Find the video in the Downloads folder.
  3. Right-click the file and select Save to Google Drive, or drag it to the Google Drive folder in the sidebar.

Chrome OS vs Linux on Chromebook

You don't need to enable Linux (Crostini) to use TikItDown. The standard Chrome browser handles everything. Linux mode is useful for running desktop apps, but TikItDown is a web tool that works in any Chrome browser on Chrome OS. Stick with the regular Chrome browser for the simplest experience.

Chromebook tips

  • Android apps - some Chromebook users install the TikTok Android app from the Play Store. TikItDown works the same way regardless - copy the link from the app and paste it in Chrome.
  • Offline access - once downloaded, you can watch the video offline. Files in the Downloads folder are accessible without an internet connection.
  • Storage - Chromebooks often have limited local storage (32-64 GB). Consider saving videos to Google Drive if space is tight.
  • Crostini Linux - if you have Linux enabled, you can also access Downloads from the Linux terminal at ~/Downloads.
