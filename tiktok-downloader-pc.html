Where downloads are saved

By default, your browser saves downloaded files to the Downloads folder on your PC. This is usually at C:\Users\[YourName]\Downloads. You can change the download location in your browser settings:

  • Chrome - Settings > Downloads > Change location
  • Edge - Settings > Downloads > Change location
  • Firefox - Settings > General > Files and Applications > Save files to

Browser-specific tips for PC

  • Chrome - downloads appear in the download bar at the bottom of the window. Click the arrow to open the file or show in folder.
  • Edge - downloads appear in a flyout at the top-right corner. Click the folder icon to open the file location.
  • Firefox - a download popup appears in the top-right. Click the folder icon to open the Downloads folder.
  • Drag and drop - you can drag the downloaded file from the browser's download bar directly into any folder or application.
