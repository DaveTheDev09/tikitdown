What is 480p Standard Definition?

480p refers to a video resolution of 854x480 pixels. It is also known as Standard Definition or SD. While it does not have the sharpness of 720p HD or 1080p Full HD, it produces much smaller files that are ideal for situations where bandwidth or storage is a concern.

480p was the standard definition for DVD video and remains widely used for mobile video streaming when connection speeds are limited. On a phone screen, 480p content is clear and watchable, though you may notice some softness compared to higher resolutions.

When to use 480p

  • Slow internet connections - when you are on a weak mobile signal or congested Wi-Fi, 480p downloads quickly without buffering.
  • Low storage situations - when your phone is running out of space, 480p files take up a fraction of the room.
  • Quick previews - when you just want to see what a video is about before committing to a larger download.
  • Messaging limits - many chat apps have file size limits. A 480p TikTok easily fits within WhatsApp, Telegram, or iMessage constraints.
  • Data saving - when you are on a limited data plan, 480p uses significantly less data than higher resolutions.

File size benefits

480p files are dramatically smaller than higher resolutions:

  • 15-second clip: 3-5 MB
  • 30-second video: 6-10 MB
  • 60-second video: 10-20 MB
  • 3-minute video: 30-60 MB

Compared to 1080p files that can be 4-5 times larger, 480p lets you save many more videos on the same device storage. A 16 GB phone that holds about 200 minutes of 1080p TikToks could store over 800 minutes at 480p.
