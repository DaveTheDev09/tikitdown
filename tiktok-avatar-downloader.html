Getting full-size avatars

When you view a TikTok profile, the avatar is shown as a small circle - typically around 100 pixels on screen. But the actual image file stored by TikTok is much larger. TikItDown retrieves the full-size source image, not the small preview you see on the profile page. This gives you the highest quality version available.

If the creator uploaded a 1080x1080 photo as their profile picture, that's the size you get. If they uploaded a 400x400 image, that's what's available. The resolution depends entirely on what the user originally uploaded.

Profile pic vs avatar

On TikTok, the terms "profile picture," "profile pic," and "avatar" all refer to the same thing - the image displayed at the top of a user's profile. There is no separate avatar system like on some other platforms. When you change your profile picture, you're changing your avatar. When someone says "save their TikTok avatar," they mean the profile picture.

Resolution and format

Avatars are downloaded as JPEG or PNG files, depending on the format the user uploaded. The image is saved at the original resolution without any cropping or compression applied by TikItDown. TikTok typically displays avatars in a circular crop, but the source file is the complete image.
