Music vs sound vs audio on TikTok

TikTok uses three terms for audio content, and understanding the difference helps you know what you're downloading:

  • Music - licensed songs from TikTok's music library. These are full or partial tracks from artists that TikTok has partnered with. When you see a song name at the bottom of a video, that's TikTok music.
  • Sounds - original audio created by TikTok users. This includes voice recordings, sound effects, commentary, and any audio that isn't from TikTok's licensed library. Creators can record their own sounds and other users can reuse them.
  • Audio - the general term covering everything you hear in a TikTok video, including both music and original sounds. The video's audio track may contain music, voiceover, or a mix of both.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between TikTok music, sound, and audio?+
On TikTok, 'music' refers to licensed songs from artists, 'sounds' are original audio created by users (voice recordings, effects, commentary), and 'audio' is the general term for all sound content. TikItDown can download all three types.
What format is the audio saved in?+
TikTok audio is typically saved as an MP3 or M4A file depending on the source. Music tracks from TikTok's library may be in M4A format while original sounds are usually MP3. Both are standard audio formats compatible with any device.
Can I download just the audio from a video?+
Yes. TikItDown can extract the audio track from any TikTok video without downloading the video itself. Paste the video link and select the audio download option.
Can I use downloaded TikTok music commercially?+
Licensed music from TikTok's library is typically restricted to use within the TikTok platform. Using it commercially outside TikTok may require separate licensing. Original sounds created by users have their own copyright - always check with the creator before using their audio commercially.
Is there a quality difference between music and sound downloads?+
Both music and sound downloads are saved at the quality TikTok provides, which is typically 128kbps or 192kbps AAC. Original sounds may vary depending on the creator's recording quality.
