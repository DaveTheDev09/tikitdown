TikTok Downloader for Safari - Apple Browser Guide

Download TikTok videos in Safari on Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Works natively - no extensions, no plugins, no configuration needed.

How to download TikTok videos in Safari on Mac

  1. Open Safari and go to TikTok to find the video you want.
  2. Copy the URL from the address bar or use Share > Copy Link.
  3. Open a new tab and go to TikItDown.
  4. Paste the link and click Download.
  5. The video will download to your Downloads folder.

How to download TikTok videos in Safari on iPhone/iPad

  1. Open the TikTok app and copy the video link.
  2. Switch to Safari and open TikItDown.
  3. Paste the link and tap Download.
  4. When prompted, tap Download in the Safari dialog.
  5. The video saves to the Files app in the Downloads folder.

Safari downloads manager

On Mac: Click the downloads icon (circle with downward arrow) in the Safari toolbar to see active and completed downloads. You can also press Option+Command+L to open the Downloads folder in Finder.

On iPhone/iPad: Safari on iOS doesn't have a visible downloads button during download. Instead, go to the Files app and check the Downloads folder. You can also tap the aA icon in the address bar and select Downloads to see the list.

Safari settings for downloads

  • Allow downloads - Safari > Settings > Websites > Downloads. Make sure TikItDown is set to "Allow".
  • Content blockers - if you use an ad blocker or content blocker extension, it may interfere with the download button. Try disabling it temporarily.
  • Private browsing - downloads work in Private mode but files are deleted when you close the private tab.
  • Request Desktop Site - if the mobile version looks off, tap aA in the address bar and select Request Desktop Website.

Safari vs Chrome for TikTok downloads

Safari on Mac and Chrome both work well for downloading TikTok videos. Safari is lighter on battery and integrates better with Apple ecosystem features like AirDrop. Chrome has a more visible download bar. Choose whichever browser you prefer - TikItDown works the same in both. See our Chrome guide for Chrome-specific tips.
