Bulk vs batch - what's the difference?

Both tools download multiple TikTok videos at once, but they're designed for different scales:

  • Batch downloader - handles up to 20 links per session. Ideal for casual users grabbing a handful of videos.
  • Bulk downloader - handles hundreds of links in a single session. Built for content researchers, social media managers, and archivists who work with large datasets.

Both tools deliver the same quality: clean, watermark-free videos in their original resolution. The bulk downloader simply removes the 20-link ceiling and adds better progress tracking for large jobs.

Use cases for bulk downloading

Content archiving

Creators delete accounts, videos get removed, and trends disappear. A bulk download lets you preserve an entire creator's body of work before it vanishes. This is especially valuable for documenting viral trends, cultural moments, or educational content that might not be reposted elsewhere.

Content research and analysis

Marketing teams and researchers need to analyze large volumes of TikTok content. Bulk downloading gives you a local library of videos you can categorize, tag, and study without constantly switching back to TikTok.

Backup and personal archives

If you've spent months building a TikTok collection or saving favorites, a bulk download lets you back up everything at once. Your personal library stays on your device, safe from deleted accounts or removed videos.
