What is the TikTok watermark?

The TikTok watermark is a small, semi-transparent logo and the creator's username that bounces around the video frame. It is added automatically when a video is uploaded to TikTok or saved through the app's built-in save feature.

The watermark serves two purposes for TikTok: it brands the content so viewers know where it came from, and it discourages reposting on other platforms without attribution. However, for personal use — saving a favorite clip, sharing with friends, or editing a compilation — the watermark gets in the way.

Why you want to remove it

The watermark covers part of the video frame. In tutorials, it often blocks on-screen text. In travel or recipe videos, it hides details near the edges. When you share a clip in a group chat, the TikTok branding makes it look like a screen recording rather than a clean video file.
