What is audio extraction?

Audio extraction takes the sound track from a TikTok video and saves it as a standalone audio file. Instead of downloading the full video, you get just the audio - whether it's a trending song snippet, an original voiceover, a comedy bit, or a sound effect someone created.

TikTok is built around audio. Sounds drive trends, creators build entire content strategies around specific tracks, and some of the best audio clips are buried in videos you'd never expect. Audio extraction lets you pull that sound out and save it as a file you can use anywhere.

Can I extract audio from any TikTok video?+
Yes. As long as the video is public, you can extract its audio. This includes both original sounds created by the uploader and sounds borrowed from other creators.
What about TikTok sounds vs original audio?+
TikTok sounds are short clips from popular songs. When you extract audio, you get whatever audio track is in the video - whether it is a trending sound, original speech, or background music.
Is extracting audio legal?+
Extracting audio for personal use such as ringtones or private listening is generally fine. Using copyrighted music commercially without permission is not allowed. Always respect copyright.

Only if the audio is original content from the creator or is licensed for reuse. Using someone else's copyrighted music without permission may violate copyright law.
