Understanding TikTok photo post types

TikTok has a few different ways creators share photos, and they work slightly differently:

Carousel posts

These are the multi-image posts where you swipe left to see the next photo. Creators use these for photo dumps, step-by-step tutorials, before-and-after comparisons, recipe ingredients, or travel photo collections. A carousel can contain anywhere from 2 to 35 images. TikItDown extracts every image in the carousel so you can save the complete set.

Slideshow posts

A slideshow is similar to a carousel but plays automatically like a video, with images transitioning one after another. These often have background music. Single photo posts

Some creators post a single image - a meme, a quote graphic, a product photo, or an announcement. TikItDown handles these the same way: paste the link, download the image at full resolution.
