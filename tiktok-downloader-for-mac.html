How to Download TikTok Videos on Mac

The complete guide to saving TikTok videos on your Mac — works in Safari, Chrome, and any browser.

Why TikItDown Works Perfectly on Mac

Mac users often worry that web-based tools won't work well on macOS, but TikItDown is built to work flawlessly on any Mac. Whether you have a MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac, or Mac Mini, TikItDown runs in your browser with zero compatibility issues.

Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Brave all handle TikItDown downloads perfectly on macOS. The tool is designed to take advantage of how macOS handles downloads, so the process is smooth and intuitive from start to finish.

Because TikItDown is browser-based, there is no need to worry about App Store restrictions, Gatekeeper warnings, or third-party app compatibility. You open the website, use it, and close the tab. Nothing gets installed on your Mac.

Step-by-Step: Download TikTok Videos on Mac

Step 1 — Copy the TikTok Link

Open TikTok in Safari, Chrome, or the TikTok desktop app on your Mac. Find the video you want to save, click the Share button, and select Copy Link. You can also select the URL in the address bar and press Cmd+C to copy it.

Short links like vm.tiktok.com/… work just as well as full URLs. TikItDown handles all link formats automatically.

Step 2 — Paste into TikItDown

Open a new tab in your browser and go to the TikItDown website. Click the download input field and press Cmd+V to paste the link. Click the Download button to start processing.

TikItDown will analyze the link and show you the video thumbnail, quality options, and available formats within seconds.

Step 3 — Save to Your Mac

Click the Download Video button. Safari will show a download confirmation — click Allow to confirm. The file will download to your default Downloads folder.

In Chrome or Firefox, the download starts immediately and you can see the progress in the download bar at the bottom of the window. Click the file when done to open it in QuickTime Player.

Where Do Downloads Go on Mac?

On macOS, browser downloads go to the Downloads folder by default. Here is how to find your downloaded TikTok videos:

  1. Open Finder.
  2. Click Downloads in the sidebar, or press Cmd+Shift+L to open it directly.
  3. Your TikTok video will be there as an MP4 file.

You can change the default download location in your browser. In Safari, go to Safari > Settings > General and select a different folder. In Chrome, go to Settings > Downloads and click Change next to the location field.

Organizing Downloads on Mac

macOS has excellent file management tools that make organizing your TikTok downloads easy:

  • Finder Tags: Right-click a video, select tags, and color-code your collection by category
  • Smart Folders: Create a Smart Folder in Finder that automatically collects all MP4 files from your Downloads
  • Stacks: Enable Stacks in Finder to automatically group files by type or date
  • Spotlight: Press Cmd+Space and search for any video by name

Tips for Mac Users

Use Safari's Download Manager

Safari on macOS has a built-in download manager accessible from the toolbar. Click the downloads icon (circle with arrow) in the top-right corner to see all active and recent downloads. You can also press Cmd+Option+L to open the download list.

Preview Before Downloading

macOS supports Quick Look for video files. After downloading, select the video in Finder and press Space to preview it without opening a media player. This is useful for quickly checking if you downloaded the right video.

AirDrop to iPhone or iPad

If you want to watch the video on your iPhone or iPad, use AirDrop. Open Finder, select the downloaded video, click the Share button, and choose AirDrop. Select your iPhone or iPad from the list, and the video will transfer wirelessly.

Save Directly to iCloud Drive

If you use iCloud Drive, you can set your download location to an iCloud folder. This makes your TikTok downloads available across all your Apple devices automatically. Go to your browser's download settings and select an iCloud Drive folder.

Saving TikTok Photos on Mac

TikItDown supports downloading TikTok photo slideshows on Mac. When you paste a photo post link, TikItDown extracts each image at full resolution without watermarks.

The images save to your Downloads folder and can be opened in Preview, Photos, or any image viewer. You can also drag them directly into other apps like Keynote, Pages, or design tools. For more details, visit our TikTok photo downloader page.

Extracting TikTok Audio on Mac

The TikItDown MP3 feature lets you extract audio from TikTok videos directly on your Mac. Paste the video link, select the MP3 option, and the audio file downloads to your Downloads folder.

You can play it in Apple Music, import it into GarageBand, or use it in video editing projects in Final Cut Pro or iMovie. The MP3 format is universally compatible with macOS apps.

Common Mac Download Issues and Fixes

Safari Blocking Downloads

If Safari is not downloading the file, check Safari > Settings > Websites > Downloads and make sure the TikItDown domain is allowed. You may also need to allow pop-ups temporarily for the download to work.

File Opens in Browser Instead of Downloading

Sometimes Safari plays the video in the browser instead of downloading it. To force a download, right-click the download button and select Download Linked File. This tells Safari to save the file rather than play it.

Download Appears as .mp4.part

If you see a .part file in your Downloads, the download was interrupted. Delete the partial file and try downloading again. This usually happens with slow internet connections or if your Mac went to sleep during the download.
