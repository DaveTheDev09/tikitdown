How to Download TikTok Photos - Complete Guide

TikTok isn't just video anymore. Millions of creators now post photo carousels, single images and slideshows. This guide walks you through saving every type of TikTok photo to your phone or computer - original quality, no watermark, no app required.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

What are TikTok photo posts?

TikTok added photo mode in 2022 and it has grown fast. Instead of a video clip, creators post a set of still images that users swipe through, often with background music playing. You will see two main formats:

  • Photo carousels - multiple images in one post, similar to an Instagram carousel. The viewer swipes left or right to see each photo.
  • Single photo posts - one image posted in photo mode, usually with a caption and sometimes music.

Both types appear in your For You Page, search results and creator profiles just like regular videos. The difference is what you are actually looking at: high-resolution images instead of a video file.

Why save TikTok photos?

Creative inspiration, design references, outfit ideas, travel photography, memes - there are plenty of reasons to want a local copy. TikTok does not offer a native save button for individual photos in a carousel, which is why tools like TikItDown's photo downloader exist.

How to download TikTok photos step by step

Step 1: Copy the post link

Open the TikTok post with the photos you want. Tap the Share arrow icon and select Copy link. The link will look something like https://www.tiktok.com/@username/video/1234567890. This works for photo posts too - TikTok uses the same URL structure.

Step 2: Paste into TikItDown

Open TikItDown in your browser. Paste the copied link into the input field and tap the Download button. TikItDown will fetch the post and show you all available images.

Step 3: Save the photos

You will see thumbnails of every image in the carousel. Tap any individual photo to download it, or use the download-all option if you want the complete set. The images save to your device's default download location at full resolution.

Downloading on iPhone

Safari on iPhone handles this the same way. Paste the link, tap download, then press and hold the image and select Save to Photos or Add to Photos. The image lands in your camera roll at full quality.

If you are using Chrome on iOS, the process is identical - long-press the image and choose the save option.

Downloading on Android

Chrome, Samsung Internet, or any Android browser works. Paste the link, download, then tap and hold the image to get the save option. Downloaded images go to your Downloads folder or Gallery depending on your browser settings.

Downloading on PC or Mac

Open TikItDown in your browser, paste the link and download. On desktop you can right-click any image and select Save image as to choose exactly where it goes. The images are standard JPEG or PNG files that open in any image viewer.

Carousel vs single photo: what's the difference?

When you paste a carousel link into TikItDown, it detects all images in the post and presents them as individual downloads. A single photo post works the same way - you just get one image instead of several. The download process is identical regardless.

The main thing to know: a carousel can contain anywhere from 2 to 35 images, and TikItDown will list every one. You choose which to save.

Image quality and file format

TikTok compresses uploaded images slightly, but the quality is generally very good - most photos are 1080 pixels wide or higher. TikItDown downloads the original file as served by TikTok's CDN, so you get the same resolution the creator uploaded. The format is typically JPEG for photos.

For reference, a typical TikTok photo download is between 100 KB and 800 KB depending on the image content and complexity.

Common issues and fixes

  • Post is private - only public posts can be downloaded. If the creator has a private account, their photos are not accessible.
  • Link not working - make sure you copied the full URL from the share menu, not just text from the caption.
  • Low quality result - TikTok sometimes serves lower quality images to older devices. Try opening TikItDown in desktop mode for higher resolution files.
