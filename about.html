About DownTik

DownTik is a free online downloader for TikTok and Douyin videos. Paste a link, press download, and get the original video - no watermark, no app, no account, no cost.

What we do

DownTik fetches the public source file of a TikTok or Douyin video and hands it to you as a clean MP4 (or MP3, or the individual photos from a slideshow). We do not re-encode, re-compress or re-render anything - the file you save is the file the creator published, minus the watermark overlay.

Why we built DownTik

Most downloader sites are wall-to-wall ads, pop-ups and fake download buttons, and many re-encode videos to cover for a slower backend. We built the tool we wanted to use: instant, clean, private and honest about what it can and cannot download. The whole flow takes seconds, works on any browser, and never asks for an account.

Our principles

  • Free. No subscription, no paywall, no hidden limits.
  • Private. No accounts, no saved download history, no tracking. Links are processed and discarded.
  • Fast. A typical video is fetched in a couple of seconds.
  • Original quality. We serve the source file, never a re-encoded copy.
  • Respectful. We support public-content downloading and recommend using downloads only for content you are allowed to reuse.
