  • Universal playback - every phone, computer, tablet, and smart TV plays MP4 natively. No codecs to install, no "format not supported" errors.
  • Messaging-friendly - WhatsApp, Telegram, iMessage, Discord, and every other messaging app handles MP4. You can send a downloaded TikTok video to anyone without worrying if they can open it.
  • Editing compatible - CapCut, iMovie, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro - they all import MP4 files without conversion.
  • Social media ready - if you want to repost a clip to Instagram, YouTube Shorts, or Twitter, MP4 is the format those platforms expect.
  • Small file size - MP4 uses efficient H.264 or H.265 compression, so a TikTok video that's 1080p and 30 seconds is usually only 3-5 MB.
