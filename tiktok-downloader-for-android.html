Why avoid APK downloader apps

Searching for a "TikTok downloader" on Android often leads to APK sites and third-party apps. That route comes with real risks:

  • Malware - APKs outside the Play Store are not scanned the way Play apps are.
  • Permission abuse - many downloader apps ask for contacts, SMS, or storage access they do not need.
  • Outdated - these apps break whenever TikTok changes its player, and then quietly stop working.
  • Ads and trackers - free downloader apps monetize with ads, and sometimes worse.

A browser-based downloader avoids every one of these issues: nothing is installed, no permissions are requested, and nothing tracks you.
