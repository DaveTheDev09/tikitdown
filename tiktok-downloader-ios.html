TikTok Downloader for iOS - iPhone and iPad Guide

Download TikTok videos on your iPhone or iPad using Safari. No app needed - works on any iOS device running iOS 13 or later.

How to download TikTok videos on iPhone

  1. Open the TikTok app and find the video you want to save.
  2. Tap Share (the arrow icon), then tap Copy link.
  3. Open Safari and go to TikItDown. Paste the link in the download field.
  4. Tap Download and pick the quality you want.
  5. When Safari asks to confirm, tap Download to save the file.

How to download TikTok videos on iPad

The process is identical on iPad. Open Safari, visit TikItDown, and follow the same steps. The video will be saved to the Files app, and you can move it to the Photos app if needed.

Where downloads go on iOS

On iOS 13 and later, Safari saves downloads to the Files app under the Downloads folder. To access your downloaded videos:

  1. Open the Files app.
  2. Tap Browse and navigate to On My iPhone or On My iPad.
  3. Open the Downloads folder to find your video.

How to save downloads to Camera Roll

  1. Open the Files app and find the downloaded video.
  2. Tap the video to open it.
  3. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  4. Select Save Video from the share sheet.
  5. The video will now appear in your Photos app.

Safari download tips for iOS

  • iOS 13 minimum - the Files app and Safari download manager require iOS 13 or later. Update through Settings > General > Software Update.
  • Download prompt - the first time you download, Safari will ask for permission. Tap Allow to continue.
  • View downloads - tap the aA icon in the Safari address bar, then tap Downloads to see your files.
  • Private Browsing - downloads still work in Private mode, but files are deleted when you close the private tab.
