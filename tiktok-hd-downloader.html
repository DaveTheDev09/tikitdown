Why quality matters for TikTok downloads

When you watch a TikTok video in the app, the player adapts to your connection speed - it might show you 480p on a slow network without you noticing. But when you download the video to keep, you want the best version available. Here's why:

  • Screen size matters - a 480p video looks fine on a 5-inch phone but looks blurry on a tablet or laptop. HD and Full HD hold up on bigger screens.
  • Editing headroom - if you're cutting a clip into a compilation or remix, starting with 1080p gives you room to crop, zoom, and reframe without losing sharpness.
  • Future-proofing - screens keep getting sharper. A 1080p video you download today will still look crisp on next year's phone.
  • Sharing quality - when you send a video to someone, the original quality transfers intact. A 480p clip re-encoded for messaging loses detail permanently.
