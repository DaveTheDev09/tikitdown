Step-by-Step: Download TikTok Videos on iPhone

Step 1 — Copy the TikTok Link

Open the TikTok app on your iPhone. Find the video you want to save, tap the Share button (the arrow icon on the right side), and choose Copy Link. The video URL is now on your clipboard.

Alternatively, if you are watching TikTok in Safari or Chrome, tap the address bar, select the URL, and copy it. Short links like vm.tiktok.com/… work just as well as full URLs.

Step 2 — Open TikItDown in Safari

Open a new tab in Safari and go to the TikItDown website. Paste the copied link into the download box using the Paste button or long-press the field and select Paste. Then tap the Download button.

TikItDown will analyze the link and prepare the download options. You will see the video thumbnail, available quality, and format options within a couple of seconds.

Step 3 — Save to Your iPhone

Tap the Download Video button. Safari will show a confirmation prompt — tap Download to confirm. The file will begin downloading immediately.

Once the download finishes, a small download icon appears in the Safari address bar. Tap it to open the file. From there, tap the Share button (the square with the arrow) and choose Save to Files to store it in the Files app, or Save Video to add it directly to your Photos library.

Where Do Downloads Go on iPhone?

One of the most common questions iPhone users have is: where does the video actually end up? Apple changed how downloads work starting with iOS 13, and many people are still not sure where to look.

On iOS 13 and later, Safari downloads go to the Files app by default. To find them:

  1. Open the Files app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Browse at the bottom if you are not already there.
  3. Tap On My iPhone.
  4. Open the Downloads folder.
  5. Your TikTok video will be there as an MP4 file.

If you want to move the video to your Photos library, open the file in the Files app, tap the Share button, and select Save Video. It will appear in your Camera Roll alongside your photos.

Common iPhone Download Issues and Fixes

Safari Asking to View in App

When you tap a TikTok link, Safari may prompt you to open it in the TikTok app. Tap Cancel or Stay in Safari to remain in the browser where TikItDown can process the link.

Download Not Starting

If the download does not start, check that Safari has permission to download files. Go to Settings > Safari > Downloads and make sure it is set to On My iPhone rather than Ask for Each Download. Also ensure you have enough free storage.

Video Not Saving to Photos

If the video downloads but does not appear in your Photos library, you need to manually save it. Open the Files app, find the video in Downloads, tap the Share button, and select Save Video. This copies it to your Camera Roll.
