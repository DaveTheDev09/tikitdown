How to download TikTok videos in Firefox

  1. Open Firefox and go to TikTok to find the video you want.
  2. Copy the URL from the address bar or use Share > Copy Link.
  3. Open a new tab and go to TikItDown.
  4. Paste the link and click Download.
  5. The video will download to your Downloads folder.

Firefox download manager

Firefox has a built-in download manager that appears as a panel in the toolbar. To access it:

  • Click the downloads icon (downward arrow) in the Firefox toolbar
  • Press Ctrl+J to open the Library window with all downloads
  • Click the folder icon next to any download to open its location in your file manager

Firefox also shows a notification bar at the bottom of the window when a download completes.

Firefox privacy features

Firefox is known for its strong privacy features, and TikItDown works perfectly with all of them:

  • Enhanced Tracking Protection - stays on by default. TikItDown is not affected by tracker blocking.
  • Total Cookie Protection - isolates cookies by site. No impact on downloads.
  • Private Browsing - downloads work in Private mode. Files are available until you close the private window.
  • Do Not Track - TikItDown respects your privacy preferences and does not track your activity.

Change download location in Firefox

  1. Open Firefox and click the hamburger menu (three horizontal lines) in the top-right.
  2. Click Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Files and Applications section.
  4. Under Downloads, change the location to your preferred folder.
  5. You can also choose to always ask where to save each file.

Firefox keyboard shortcuts

  • Ctrl+J - open the Downloads library
  • Ctrl+T - open a new tab for TikItDown
  • Ctrl+L - jump to the address bar to paste a link
  • Ctrl+Shift+Delete - clear browsing data including download history
  • Ctrl+Shift+P - open a Private window

Firefox vs other browsers for TikTok downloads
