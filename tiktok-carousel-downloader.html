What is a TikTok carousel?

TikTok carousel posts are swipeable collections of images. Instead of a single photo or a video, the creator uploads multiple pictures that viewers scroll through left to right. You'll see a small indicator at the bottom of the post showing how many images are in the set. Carousels have become popular for tutorials, before-and-after photos, recipe steps, outfit showcases, and photo dumps.

How to download TikTok carousel photos

  1. Open TikTok and find the carousel post you want to save.
  2. Tap the Share icon (the arrow), then tap Copy link.
  3. Paste the link into the TikItDown field above and press Download.
  4. TikItDown will detect the carousel and show you all available images.
  5. Download them individually or grab the entire set at once.

Mixed carousels with photos and videos

Some TikTok carousels contain a mix of still images and short video clips. Why download carousel photos instead of screenshotting?

Screenshotting a carousel means you're capturing the full TikTok UI - the username, like button, comment icon, and the carousel indicator all get included. You also get whatever screen size your device has, not the original image resolution.
