Where to find the TikTok link

On the TikTok app

  1. Open the video you want to download.
  2. Tap the Share icon (the arrow on the right side of the screen).
  3. Tap Copy link. The URL is now on your clipboard.
  4. Switch to TikItDown and paste the link.

On the TikTok website

  1. Open the video on tiktok.com.
  2. Click the Share icon below the video.
  3. Click Copy link.
  4. Paste it into TikItDown.
