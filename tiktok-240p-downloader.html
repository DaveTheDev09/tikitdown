What is 240p quality?

240p refers to a video resolution of 426x240 pixels. It is considered low quality by modern standards, producing a soft and somewhat blurry image compared to 720p or 1080p. However, the extreme compression comes with a major advantage: incredibly small file sizes.

240p is the resolution you see when YouTube automatically drops to the lowest quality on a very slow connection. While it lacks detail, it remains watchable for understanding the overall content of a video.

When low quality is useful

  • Previews - download a tiny version first to see what a video is about before committing to a larger file.
  • Testing - quickly verify that a link works and the video is what you expect.
  • Very slow connections - when you are on 2G, EDGE, or satellite internet, 240p is often the only quality that downloads without interruption.
  • Extreme storage limits - on devices with very limited storage, 240p lets you save dozens of videos in the space one 1080p video would occupy.
  • Messaging with size limits - some platforms compress or reject files over a certain size. 240p files easily fit within even the strictest limits.

File size comparison
