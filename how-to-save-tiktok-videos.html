How to Save TikTok Videos — All Methods Explained

There are several ways to save a TikTok video — some built into the app, others using third-party tools. This guide walks through every method so you can pick the one that works best for you.

Save vs. download: what's the difference?

TikTok uses the word "save" for two different things. Inside the app, tapping the bookmark icon saves the video to your favorites — it stays online and only you can see it. Tapping Share → Save video downloads a copy to your device with the watermark embedded.

Downloading through a tool like TikItDown gives you the original source file without the watermark, saved directly to your device's storage.

Method 1: TikTok's built-in save

  1. Open the TikTok app and find the video.
  2. Tap Share (the arrow icon).
  3. Tap Save video.
  4. The video downloads to your camera roll or gallery with the TikTok watermark.

This is the simplest method, but it has limitations: the watermark stays on the video, some creators disable saving, and the quality may be lower than the original upload.

Method 2: Using TikItDown (no watermark)

  1. Open the TikTok app or website and find the video.
  2. Tap Share, then tap Copy link.
  3. Paste the link into the TikItDown field above and press Download.
  4. Choose the download option. The video saves without any TikTok overlay.

This method works on any device and browser. The video is the original source file — no watermark, full quality.

Method 3: Browser download on desktop

  1. Open tiktok.com in your browser and find the video.
  2. Copy the URL from the address bar.
  3. Paste the URL into TikItDown and click Download.
  4. Right-click the download link and select Save link as... to choose where the file goes.

Method 4: Screen recording (not recommended)

You can use your device's screen recording feature to capture the video as it plays. However, this captures the watermark along with any on-screen elements like notifications and battery icons. FAQ

What is the difference between saving and downloading a TikTok video?+
Saving a TikTok video through the app adds it to your favorites within TikTok — it stays online. Downloading saves a copy of the video file to your device so you can watch it offline.
Why can't I save some TikTok videos?+
Creators can disable the save option for their videos. Additionally, private and unlisted videos cannot be saved or downloaded by anyone outside the creator's approved audience.
Where do saved TikTok videos go on my phone?+
Videos downloaded through the TikTok app are saved to your camera roll or gallery. Videos downloaded through TikItDown go to your browser's downloads folder, which you can access through your file manager.
Can I save TikTok videos without the watermark?+
Yes. Using TikItDown, you can download the original source video without the TikTok watermark. The in-app save feature always includes the watermark.
Is it legal to save TikTok videos?+
Saving a video for personal use — watching offline, sharing privately — is generally fine. Re-uploading someone else's content as your own or using it commercially without permission is not allowed.
