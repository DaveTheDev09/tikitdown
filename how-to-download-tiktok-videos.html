How to Download TikTok Videos on Any Device

You can download any public TikTok video to your phone, tablet or computer in about ten seconds: copy the link, paste it into DownTik, pick a quality and save. No watermark, no app, no account, no cost. Here is the full walkthrough for every device.

The 3-step method that works everywhere

  1. Copy the link. Open TikTok, tap Share on the video and choose Copy link. On desktop you can also just copy the URL from the address bar.
  2. Paste it into DownTik. Go to the DownTik homepage, paste the link into the field and press Download. The video is analyzed in a couple of seconds.
  3. Save the file. Pick the best quality and tap download. The video - without the TikTok watermark - saves straight to your device.

Download TikTok videos on iPhone and iPad

Open the TikTok app, tap Share on any public video, then Copy link. Switch to Safari, open the DownTik homepage, paste the link and hit Download. The MP4 lands in your Downloads, ready to move into Photos or share anywhere.

Tip: in Safari tap the Share button and choose Add to Home Screen for DownTik. Next time it behaves like an installed app - no download, no App Store.

Download TikTok videos on Android

In the TikTok app, tap Share then Copy link. Open Chrome, go to DownTik, paste the link and download. The video appears in your Downloads folder (visible in Files or your gallery's download album). Since Android handles video better than iOS, you can also save 1080p or 4K sources without issues.

Download TikTok videos on PC and Mac

On your computer, open TikTok in any browser, open the video you want and copy the URL from the address bar. Paste it into DownTik, choose quality and save. On Mac the file lands in Downloads; on Windows it lands in your default download folder. The file is a standard MP4 that plays in any player and imports into CapCut, Premiere or DaVinci Resolve.

Download TikTok photos (slideshows) without watermark

Not every TikTok post is a video. For photo slideshows, DownTik detects each image in the post and lets you save them individually in original quality - useful when you want clean HD stills for edits, mood boards or archives.

What you cannot download

  • Private or friends-only videos - TikTok does not expose a public source for them.
  • Deleted videos - once removed, the source file is gone everywhere.
  • Livestreams - live content cannot be fetched after the fact.

If your link is a normal public video and it still fails, double-check that you copied the full www.tiktok.com/@user/video/... link and not a profile URL.
