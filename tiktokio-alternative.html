What is TikTokio?

TikTokio is a web-based TikTok downloader. You paste a video link, it processes the URL, and gives you a download button for the video without the watermark. It's straightforward and does the basics well.

TikTokio gained popularity because it was one of the earlier web-based options that didn't require an app install. It has a clean-looking homepage and handles standard TikTok video links reliably.

How we tested

We measured from the moment the page finished loading to the moment the download button appeared. Tests were run on both desktop Chrome and mobile Safari to capture real-world conditions.
